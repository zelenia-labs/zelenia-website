## 1. Professional Reality & Emotional Context
Mateo/Sofia has spent years developing a singular voice and aesthetic language. While their physical work or live presence commands respect, their current digital presence is frustrating:
- **The Dignity Dilemma:** They refuse to look like a generic transactional online store. Putting discount banners or clunky "Add to Cart" widgets next to serious artistic work cheapens the craft and alienates collectors and institutional curators.
- **The Social Platform Trap:** Instagram and streaming services compress imagery and audio, crop layouts, and bury their lifelong craft under ephemeral algorithms. Serious collectors, museum directors, and high-ticket patrons do not conduct meaningful acquisitions through social media comments.
- **The Broken Template:** Their current website is built on outdated template platforms (Squarespace, Wix, or bloated WordPress). Images take 4+ seconds to load, layouts shift, typography looks unrefined, and color accuracy across screens is lost.
- **What They Actually Need:** A living digital archive and exhibition space that honors scale, texture, typography, and mood—providing discreet inquiry paths and requiring zero maintenance or plugin updates.

When evaluating website sections, copy, or interactive flows from this persona's lens:
