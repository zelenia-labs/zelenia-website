# Persona: Marcus Vance — VP of Engineering (Enterprise / Post-IPO Tech)

## Executive Summary
- **Title:** Vice President of Engineering / Head of Architecture
- **Company:** B2B Cloud / Fintech Enterprise ($120M–$500M ARR, 600+ employees, 180 engineers)
- **Reporting to:** CTO / Chief Product Officer
- **Annual Budget Authority:** $250,000 discretionary for external specialized technical sprints
- **Decision Horizon:** 2 to 4 weeks (urgent quarterly initiative or board-level mandate)

---

## 1. Professional Context & Daily Reality
Marcus manages 14 engineering pods distributed globally. His teams are 100% focused on shipping core roadmap features, data pipelines, and backend microservices. However, the client-facing web application and flagship marketing web properties have accumulated catastrophic frontend technical debt:
- Core Web Vitals (LCP, INP, CLS) are in Google's "Poor" red zone, actively dragging down organic pipeline and conversion.
- Component libraries are fragmented across 3 separate design token migrations.
- In-house Staff Engineers refuse to touch marketing or presentation architecture because they view it as "not real engineering."
- Marcus is under relentless pressure from the CMO and CEO to "make the web app fly" before the next annual customer conference.

---

## 2. Past Trauma & Negative Experiences (The Skepticism Filter)
- **The $450k Traditional Agency Fiasco:** Marcus hired a prominent global digital agency 18 months ago. During the pitch, two charismatic Senior Partners presented slick slides. Once the contract was signed, delivery was handed off to junior developers in a low-cost offshore hub who didn't understand modern Angular/React reactivity, signal graphs, or CSS containment. They delivered 45,000 lines of unmaintainable CSS, bloated webpack configs, and vanished.
- **The Freelance Flakiness:** Previous contractors left behind zero documentation, no unit tests, and tight vendor lock-in.

---

## 3. What Marcus Looks for in Zelenia
- **Direct Senior Density:** He wants to deal *only* with practitioners who match or exceed his team's technical caliber. The fact that Alejandro Cuba is an active **Fortune 100 Principal Engineer** and **Google Developer Expert (GDE)** gives Marcus instant political air cover to approve a contract without lengthy procurement committees.
- **Direct Git Commits:** No zip files, no handoff portals. Zelenia engineers commit directly to Marcus's GitHub organization with conventional commits, strict linting, and 100% test coverage.
- **Deterministic Guarantees:** Concrete SLAs—e.g. LCP < 1.0s, zero CLS, zero console errors, full WCAG 2.2 AA accessibility.
