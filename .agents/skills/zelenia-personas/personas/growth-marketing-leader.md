# Persona: Julian Thorne — VP of Marketing & Growth

## Executive Summary
- **Archetype:** Vice President of Marketing, Head of Growth, or Brand Director.
- **Company Context:** High-growth company or scale-up ($10M–$100M ARR, average deal size or customer LTV in the thousands to hundreds of thousands).
- **Discovery Channel:** Organic search, Core Web Vitals speed diagnostics, industry word of mouth, or performance benchmarks.
- **Primary Need:** A flagship web property that captures immediate executive trust, fixes failing Core Web Vitals (sub-second LCP), eliminates conversion leaks on ad spend, and commands premium pricing.
- **Investment Horizon:** $30,000–$90,000 (justified by pipeline conversion lift, lower CAC, and organic search ranking recovery).
- **Decision Cycle:** 2 to 3 weeks.

---

## 1. Professional Reality & Daily Pressures
Julian is accountable for customer acquisition costs (CAC), conversion rates, and brand memorability:
- **The Speed-to-Revenue Bleed:** Every 100ms of latency costs measurable pipeline. Their current marketing site has a 3.2-second LCP and layout shift issues that actively suppress Google organic rankings and cause paid ad traffic to bounce before engaging.
- **The Cookie-Cutter Malaise:** Most agency redesigns look like identical SaaS templates (generic centered headlines, purple gradient cards, and stock vectors). Prospective buyers visit and forget the company within seconds.
- **Internal Engineering Bottlenecks:** In-house software engineers are locked into the core product backlog and view marketing pages as low priority, leading to multi-month launch delays for critical campaign flagships.
- **What Julian Actually Needs:** A high-prestige digital flagship that loads in under 800ms globally, conveys instant market authority, and provides clear, friction-free conversion paths.

## 2. Past Negative Experiences & Skepticism
- **The "Creative Award" Trap:** Agencies that built visually flashy websites with 10MB background videos and heavy third-party scripts that looked cool in agency award submissions but crippled page speed, tanked SEO, and converted zero leads.
- **Sluggish Timelines:** Agencies requiring 6 months of bloated "discovery workshops" and layers of project managers before producing a single usable screen.
- **Fluffy Promises:** Partners promising "holistic engagement" without guaranteeing measurable speed metrics, accessibility, or conversion benchmarks.

## 4. Evaluation Rubric for Advisory Board / Agentic UX
When evaluating website sections, copy, or interactive flows from this persona's lens:
