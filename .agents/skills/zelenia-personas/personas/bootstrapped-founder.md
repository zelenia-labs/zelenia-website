## 1. Professional Context & Daily Reality
Soren is a technical or product-focused solopreneur who built a profitable vertical software product. He makes $200k/month in profit, but is competing against well-funded VC competitors with 40-person teams:
- Soren's backend and database logic are rock solid, but his landing page and user dashboard still look like an engineer's side-project from 2021.

- Soren has zero interest in hiring full-time employees, managing HR, or granting equity. He wants a **capital-efficient, high-leverage strike team** that can elevate his entire brand and user interface to unicorn standards in a single 4-week sprint.
