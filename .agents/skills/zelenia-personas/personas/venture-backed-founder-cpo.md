## 1. Professional Context & Daily Reality
Elena founded the company 2.5 years ago. The technical product works brilliantly on the backend (machine learning algorithms, real-time data ingestion, high uptime), but the user-facing interface was originally hacked together with off-the-shelf Tailwind/Bootstrap templates:
- When presenting to Tier-1 enterprise buyers (Fortune 500 CISOs, Chief Risk Officers), feedback comes back: *"The tech is powerful, but the UI looks like a prototype."*
- Elena is preparing for a crucial Series B raise in 4 months. She needs the product and website to look like a category-defining multi-billion-dollar market leader, not a fragile demo.
- She has tried hiring a full-time Principal Product Designer and Staff Frontend Architect, but recruiting in the Bay Area / NYC takes 6+ months and costs $450k/year in cash + heavy equity that she doesn't want to dilute.
