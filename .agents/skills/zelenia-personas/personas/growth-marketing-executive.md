# Persona: Julian Thorne — VP of Growth & Marketing (High-ACV B2B SaaS)

## Executive Summary
- **Title:** Vice President of Growth & Marketing / Chief Marketing Officer
- **Company:** High-ACV Enterprise B2B SaaS ($30M–$150M ARR, average contract value $40k–$200k/yr)
- **Reporting to:** CEO / CRO
- **Annual Budget Authority:** $180,000 for web performance, conversion optimization, and brand flagship overhauls
- **Decision Horizon:** 2 to 3 weeks

---

## 1. Professional Context & Daily Reality
Julian is held strictly accountable for Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), pipeline conversion rate, and pipeline velocity. He spends over $120,000/month on Google Search, LinkedIn ads, and executive outbound campaigns:
- **The Conversion Leak:** Mobile traffic bounce rates exceed 62% because the current website has a 3.4-second LCP and suffers from layout shifts (CLS) when third-party scripts load.
- **Brand Amnesia:** Prospective enterprise buyers visit the site and forget it 10 seconds later because it looks identical to every other SaaS startup in the valley (centered headlines, white card grid, purple buttons).
- Julian needs a flagship web property that loads in under 800ms globally and captures immediate emotional and rational trust.

---

## 2. Past Trauma & Negative Experiences
- **The SEO/Speed Destroyers:** Julian hired a "creative agency" that built a visually stunning Webflow/Gatsby site with uncompressed 8MB video loops and synchronous scripts that wrecked organic Google rankings and triggered Core Web Vitals penalties overnight.
- **The "Creative Ego" Trap:** Designers who prioritized their own award portfolio over Julian's actual lead capture funnels and revenue metrics.

---

## 3. What Julian Looks for in Zelenia
- **Performance is Revenue:** He understands that a 500ms drop in LCP yields a measurable 10–15% uplift in inbound enterprise demos. Zelenia's dual promise (*"We repair critical web vitals"* + *"Sub-second LCP guarantee"*) speaks directly to his core KPI.
- **High-Prestige Memorability:** A design that stops executive buyers in their tracks, feels like an elite luxury instrument, and validates a six-figure contract price point.
- **Measurable Impact:** Concrete diagnostics before and after the sprint.
