## 1. Professional Context & Daily Reality
Mateo/Elena has spent decades refining a distinct visual language. Their work commands serious respect in physical studios, galleries, and private collections, but their digital presence is an agonizing paradox:
- **The Dignity Dilemma:** They vehemently refuse to look like a generic "e-commerce shop." Putting an "Add to Cart" button or a discount banner next to a $25,000 oil canvas or bronze sculpture feels culturally toxic and cheapens the work.
- **The Instagram Trap:** While Instagram provides social buzz, serious collectors, art advisors, and museum curators do not make $50,000 acquisitions via DMs. Instagram compresses images, crops aspect ratios, and subjects their lifelong craft to transient algorithmic noise.
- **The Amateurish Portfolio:** Their current site is either nonexistent or built on an outdated Squarespace/Wix/WordPress template. Images take 4 seconds to load, colors look washed out or distorted on different screens, navigation is clumsy, and there is no sense of spatial atmosphere.
- **What They Actually Crave:** A **Bespoke Digital Atelier and Living Archive (Catalog Raisonné)**—a digital exhibition space that honors the scale, texture, and philosophy of the work, provides discreet collector acquisition funnels, and requires zero technical maintenance.
