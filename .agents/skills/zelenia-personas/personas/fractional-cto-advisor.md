## 1. Professional Context & Daily Reality
Gregory is a former VP of Engineering / CTO at a successful unicorn who now operates as an independent Fractional CTO. His clients bring him in for 10–20 hours a month to solve strategic technical bottlenecks:
- In almost every portfolio company he audits, he identifies severe frontend execution problems: slow time-to-market, broken mobile experiences, fragile CSS, and failing Core Web Vitals.
- Gregory does not have the bandwidth to write production code himself—his value is architectural governance, hiring strategy, and vendor selection.
- When his clients need a high-risk web application rebuilt or a flagship web asset delivered before an enterprise pilot, the client asks Gregory: *"Who should we hire to build this? Do you know a team that won't screw this up?"*
- Gregory puts his personal reputation on the line with every vendor recommendation. If an agency drops the ball or writes junior code, Gregory's client loses trust in him.
